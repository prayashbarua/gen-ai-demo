Call with Linda Alvarez — June 19, 2026

Linda: My granddaughter just got into college, and I'd love to help with tuition. Is there a smart way to do that without messing up my own taxes?

Advisor: That's a lovely thing to do, and yes, there are a few good options. One is to contribute directly to a 529 plan in her name — you can gift up to the annual exclusion amount per year without any gift tax filing. Another option, if you want to help with tuition specifically, is paying the school directly, which doesn't count against the gift tax exclusion at all.

Linda: Paying the school directly sounds simple. Would that come out of my IRA?

Advisor: We'd want to avoid pulling directly from your IRA if possible, since withdrawals are taxable income to you. It would be better to use funds from your taxable brokerage account, and we can plan the timing so it doesn't create an unnecessarily large tax bill in one year.

Linda: That's a relief, I was worried this would complicate things.

Advisor: Not at all — let's put together a simple gifting plan for the next few years so you can support her without any surprises at tax time.
